# 🌱 AgriN – Regenerative Agricultural Intelligence

AgriN is an AI-powered regenerative agriculture platform designed to help farmers improve soil health, conserve water, increase biodiversity, reduce agricultural waste, and build climate-resilient sources of income.

## 🚜 Key Features

- 🤖 AI Farm Copilot
- 🌾 Crop Intelligence
- 🔄 Regenerative Crop Planning
- 💧 Water Intelligence
- ♻️ Waste-to-Value AI
- 🌳 Biodiversity Planning
- 📸 AI Crop Doctor
- 🧪 Soil Scanner
- 🏘️ Panchayat Intelligence
- 🌡️ Climate Resilience
- 💰 Regenerative ROI Calculator
- 📅 30-Day Regeneration Challenge
- 🪪 Digital Farm Passport
- 📊 Impact Dashboard
- 🌦️ Weather Intelligence

## 🎯 Problem

Farmers often focus on short-term crop yield while facing soil degradation, water scarcity, climate risks, crop diseases, and agricultural waste.

AgriN provides an integrated intelligence platform that focuses on the long-term regeneration of the farm.

## 💡 Our Approach

AgriN follows a continuous regeneration cycle:

**Observe → Diagnose → Recommend → Act → Measure → Learn → Improve**

## 🗺️ Target Region

The prototype focuses on agricultural conditions in **North Karnataka**, with Panchayat-level intelligence designed for areas such as Kalaburagi.
